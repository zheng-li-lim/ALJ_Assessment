# ABC Mobility Intelligence

A fortnightly automotive briefing tool built for ABC's Chief Strategy Officer and Chairman. It surfaces the news that matters — filtered by geography, classified by urgency, and written for a 15-minute read.

---

## What It Does

Each edition covers the Middle East and European automotive markets. News items are categorised by topic (Regulations, Competition, Product Innovation, etc.) and assigned an action type — FYI, Assess, or Act & Escalate — so the reader knows immediately what requires attention.

Key features:
- **Fortnightly editions** with an executive summary at the top
- **Filters** by geography, topic, and urgency level
- **Fact check** and **CSO review** flags on individual items
- **European car sales data** tab with market context
- **Email export** — one click to generate a formatted newsletter

---

## How to Run It

1. Clone or download the repository
2. In your terminal, run:
   ```bash
   node serve.mjs
   ```
3. Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser

No installation or configuration required beyond that.

---

## How to Read an Edition

- The **Executive Summary** at the top gives you the key takeaway in 3–4 sentences
- Each card shows the headline, geography, source, and a **"So what for ABC?"** panel
- The coloured badge on each card tells you the recommended action: grey = FYI, gold = Assess, red = Act & Escalate
- Use the filter bar to narrow by region or topic
- Click **Share as Email** to export the current view as a formatted newsletter

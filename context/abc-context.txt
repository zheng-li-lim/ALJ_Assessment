## Strategic Priorities

1. **EV transition management** — Toyota ICE dominance shifting to EV. Dealer network, technician training, charging infrastructure partnerships across all Toyota markets.
2. **Chinese EV distribution expansion** — Active in Egypt (GAC, BYD), UAE (Farizon), Italy (Zeekr), Poland (GAC likely), UK (Farizon CV). 11-country Farizon agreement not yet fully public.
3. **Protecting KSA Toyota market share** — ~50% KSA share under pressure from BYD direct entry and Vision 2030 EV incentives.
4. **Aftermarket growth** — EV servicing capabilities. TADME moat with Toyota/Lexus accessories.
5. **Financing conditions** — SAMA (KSA), TCMB (Turkey), CBE (Egypt) rates affect vehicle affordability and ABC loan volumes directly.
